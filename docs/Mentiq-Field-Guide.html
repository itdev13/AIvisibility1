§1 · The idea

When buyers ask AI, our clients should be the answer.

More and more buyers ask an AI assistant "who should I use for…" instead of searching Google. The AI names three to five businesses. If a business isn't one of them, the buyer never sees it. We sell a done-for-you service that tracks those answers every day and does the work to get our client named: content, site fixes and off-site mentions.

We sit between expensive agencies ($3k–8k a month, slow) and cheap tracking tools ($30–500 a month, but the client does all the work). Our price is $499–2,499 a month, month to month.

Three groups of people use one app

Prospects

The public website

They read about Mentiq, run a free AI visibility check on their own site, and leave their email to unlock the full report. That makes them a lead for James.

Paying clients

The client dashboard

They see how often AI recommends them, against which competitors, and everything we did for them this month. They approve our content before it goes live.

James & Srinivas

The Ops desk

Our internal console. Leads, clients, revenue, the content editing queue, and the background jobs that do the work.

A client's journey, start to finish

  1. A business owner runs the free check on our website and sees which AI answers leave them out.
  2. They unlock the full report with their email and become a lead.
  3. James calls them, or they buy online. They pick a plan and pay.
  4. They describe their business. Our site scan fills most of it in.
  5. Within about an hour the app writes their buyer questions, audits their site, runs the first AI check and drafts a 90-day plan.
  6. Every day after that, it asks every question on every AI engine in their plan and records who gets named.
  7. Every month it drafts their articles. Our editor fact-checks each one, the client approves it, and it's published to their site.

§6 · Automation

What runs automatically

A background "worker" runs next to the website and does all the slow AI work. If the worker isn't running, nothing new happens. Every night at 05:00 UTC it schedules each active client's work:

WorkHow oftenWhat happens
AI answer checkDailyEvery active question on every engine in the plan. Results roll up into that day's score.
Traffic syncDailyPulls Search Console and GA4 numbers, if connected.
Site auditOnce, monthly or weeklyDepends on plan: Starter once, Growth monthly, Scale weekly.
Content plan and draftsMonthlyFills the month's quota and drafts each piece for editing.
Off-site opportunitiesWeekly (Mondays)Scale only. Finds new Reddit and Quora threads.
